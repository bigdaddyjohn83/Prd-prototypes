THE DIFFERENCE

You know the trade.
The DEX Venue is what changed.

Funding rates, liquidation and leverage tiers work the way you already expect. What is different is who holds your collateral, where the order book actually lives, what happens when the operator goes down, and whether anyone can prove the fill you got was fair.

🔑

You hold the keys

Collateral sits in a wallet or contract you control. Nobody can freeze it, and nobody can restore it for you either.

⛓

The order book has an address

Matching runs on a chain, a rollup or a sequencer. Where it runs decides what can be proven and what has to be trusted.

≈

Fees look lower, cost is not always lower

A zero fee headline can still cost more once depth, slippage, funding and gas are counted into the round trip.

Funding Path

Getting funds from an exchange to a DEX

None of these DEX Venues accept fiat, in any currency. The route runs through a licensed exchange in your own market, and the withdrawal step is the part that catches people out.

1

Buy or hold stablecoins on a licensed exchange

Any licensed exchange that supports a bank account in your currency. Your funds start here, not in a wallet.

Withdraw to a wallet you control
